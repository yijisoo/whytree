# Reading Recommendations (Product)

Offer at most one per session, conversationally, at a natural pause (after a Phase 5 reflection or when the builder asks "where do I go from here?"). **Never interrupt discovery to recommend reading.**

### "How to Do Great Work" — Paul Graham
**URL:** https://paulgraham.com/greatwork.html
**Suggest when:** The builder has surfaced a vision but is unsure how to aim it, or is torn between what's impressive and what genuinely interests them. The essay's argument that curiosity is a more reliable compass than ambition speaks directly to product direction.
**One-line pitch:** *"Paul Graham's 'How to Do Great Work' — especially the idea that what you're genuinely curious about is a better compass than what looks ambitious. Worth a read for where your tree is pointing."*

### "Boil the Ocean" — Garry Tan
**URL:** https://garryslist.org/posts/boil-the-ocean
**Suggest when:** The builder's How Downs are incremental and safe, or fear of scope ("that's too ambitious for us") is capping the feature exploration.
**One-line pitch:** *"There's a short piece by Garry Tan, 'Boil the Ocean' — about expanding ambition rather than protecting what you have. Might fit where your product tree is heading."*

### "Tiny Experiments" — Anne-Laure Le Cunff (TEDx Nashville)
**URL:** https://nesslabs.com/tiny-experiments-tedx-nashville-transcript
**Suggest when:** The builder reaches the Commitment Arc and feels the gap between the vision and what's shippable now. Reframes each next feature as an experiment that teaches regardless of outcome.
**One-line pitch:** *"Anne-Laure Le Cunff's 'Tiny Experiments' — every release is an experiment that tells you something whether it lands or not. Fifteen minutes, fits the close."*

## How to offer
- One reading per session, framed as optional. The session's own discoveries are always primary.
